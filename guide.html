Your First Tool

The 10-Second Pause

Between what happens to you and what you do next, there is a space. When the pattern runs, that space is a fraction of a second. It fires before you even see it coming. The work is to widen that space until there's room for a choice. Start here.

01

Notice the cue

Learn the physical signal that your pattern is about to fire: a tight chest, a held breath, a clenched jaw, a flush of heat. The body knows before the mind does.

02

Do nothing for ten seconds

When you feel it, don't respond. Don't send the text, say the thing, or take the action. Just breathe, slow out-breaths. Ten seconds. That's the whole drill.

03

Ask one question

In the space you just made, ask: "Is this a fact, or a story?" Then choose your response from there, not from the pattern.

THE BODY KNOWS FIRST
SLOW OUT-BREATHS. COUNT THEM.
Try It This Week

Pick one recurring trigger: a certain email, a certain person, a certain time of day. For one week, run the 10-second pause every time it shows up. Notice what changes.
